A QR code is what you put in front of customers so they can take part in your loyalty program. You print it, stand it on a table, or stick it by the till. A scan from a plain phone camera opens a join page, so somebody who has never heard of Qtap can walk out as a member of your program.

## Show the code

Open **QR Codes** in the sidebar and click the code you want to display. Its detail page has a preview of the code and two download buttons.

<Frame caption="A code's preview, its scan address, and the PNG and SVG download buttons.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/qr-codes/customer-scan-show-code.png" alt="QR code preview card with the scan URL and PNG and SVG download buttons" />
</Frame>

- **PNG** suits a screen or a quick print on standard paper.
- **SVG** stays sharp at any size, so use it for a window decal or a large poster.

The text under the image is the code's scan address: `dashboard.qtap.qa/scan/` followed by the code value. That is the page a scan opens, which is why any phone can read it. Put the printed code where customers already pause, like the counter, the table, or the receipt.

## What the customer sees

The page opens with your business name and the card they are about to start, so they know whose program this is before they type anything.

<Frame caption="Your name (1) and the card the scan starts (2) sit at the top of the join page.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/qr-codes/customer-scan-join.png" alt="Join page on a phone showing Welcome to Brew and Bean Cafe above a stamp card preview reading 0 of 5" />
</Frame>

A points code shows the points card instead, with your earn rate and the first reward on it.

<Frame caption="The same page for a points code, showing the earn rate and the next reward.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/qr-codes/customer-scan-join-points.png" alt="Join page showing a points card with the line Earn 1 point for every QAR 1 spent and Next reward at 50 points" />
</Frame>

## What they fill in

Only the phone number and the terms box are required. Everything else is there to make the card worth more later.

<Frame caption="Phone (1) is the only detail Qtap needs. Birthday (2) is optional. Get my card (3) submits.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/qr-codes/customer-scan-join-form.png" alt="Join form showing optional full name, required phone with country picker, optional birthday, terms checkbox, marketing checkbox and the Get my card button" />
</Frame>

The country picker starts on the country Qtap detects and can be changed. Birthday carries the line "Get a reward on it", which is what makes a customer eligible for a [birthday campaign](/merchants/campaigns/birthday). The second checkbox, for offers from your business, is optional and unticked.

Tapping **Get my card** shows the number back before anything is saved.

<Frame caption="The number is shown once more, with Edit number (1) and Confirm and join (2).">
  <img src="/images/merchants/qr-codes/customer-scan-confirm.png" alt="Dialog reading Confirm your phone number above a Qatar number with Edit number and Confirm and join buttons" />
</Frame>

<Note>
No code is sent to that phone. Qtap checks the shape of the number, not the person holding it, so the number stays unconfirmed until they install the Qtap app and claim the card. Your members list marks these people so your staff know.
</Note>

## What the scan is worth

Confirming lands on one of three endings, and only the first one earns anything.

<Frame caption="The same code produces a different ending depending on who scanned it.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/qr-codes/customer-scan-paths.svg" alt="Diagram showing a scan leading to joined, welcome back, or already has the app" />
</Frame>

- **New to your business.** They become a member and that first scan runs the same earning rules a staff scan does, so a stamp or the points land straight away. On a stamp or points code they are offered a wallet pass, and it arrives already showing what they just earned rather than an empty card.
- **Already a member with you.** The page says welcome back and offers their pass again in case they lost it. Nothing is earned. Repeat earning happens at the counter, where a staff member scans them.
- **Already using the Qtap app.** The page points them at the app, where the card already is. Nothing is earned here.

<Warning>
A sign-up reward, a completed card, or a campaign reward earned during that first scan is saved to the customer's account but never shown on the join page. Rewards appear once they install the app, so a customer can be carrying one they have not been told about. See [Customers who joined without the app](/merchants/members/joined-without-the-app) for what that looks like on your side of the counter.
</Warning>

<Note>
A check-in code joins the customer and records the visit without adding stamps or points, by design. Use it for a code by the door or at an event where you want attendance rather than rewards.
</Note>

Only a scan that ends in a join spends the code: it adds to the scan total, counts toward any limit you set, and closes a one-time code. Someone who opens the page and walks away costs you nothing.

## When a code cannot be used

A code that has been deleted, switched off, passed its expiry date, or used up says so in place of the form.

<Frame caption="A code Qtap cannot find shows this instead of the join page.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/qr-codes/customer-scan-notvalid.png" alt="Error card reading This QR code is not valid, check that you scanned the merchant's current loyalty card QR code" />
</Frame>

The other three read "This QR code is inactive", "This QR code has expired", and "This QR code has reached its limit". [Troubleshooting](/merchants/qr-codes/troubleshooting) covers how to tell which one you are looking at and how to fix it.

## Redeeming a reward

Scanning a code adds stamps or points. Claiming a reward is a separate step. When a customer is ready to use a reward they earned, a staff member handles it on the Redemptions screen, where you confirm the reward and the balance is settled.
