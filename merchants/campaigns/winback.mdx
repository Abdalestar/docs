---
title: "Win-Back Campaigns"
description: "Automatically message members who haven't visited in a while, with a reward code attached to bring them back."
---

A win-back campaign watches for members who stopped coming in. After a quiet stretch you choose, Qtap sends them a push notification. Attach a reward and the notification carries a redemption code they can use on their next visit.

## How it works

Qtap checks your active campaigns every 15 minutes. A member qualifies when their last recorded visit is older than the inactivity window you set. If the campaign has a reward, Qtap issues it first and puts the code inside the message, so the member never has to go hunting for it.

A visit means any activity on your loyalty program: earning stamps or points (from a scan or from staff), or redeeming a reward code. Members who joined but never earned anything have no recorded visit, so they count as inactive and will get the message too.

Members who turned off push, or muted your business, don't get the push itself. The campaign still lands in their inbox inside the app.

## Setting up a win-back campaign

Go to **Campaigns** in the left sidebar and click **New Campaign**.

<Frame caption="The Campaigns page. Click New Campaign to open the wizard.">
  <img src="/images/campaigns/winback-01-list.png" alt="Campaigns list with the New Campaign button highlighted" />
</Frame>

<Steps>

### Choose the type

Select **Win-Back Campaign** and give it a name you'll recognize later, like "We Miss You". The description is optional and only your team sees it.

<Frame caption="Choose Win-Back Campaign and enter a campaign name.">
  <img src="/images/campaigns/winback-02-type.png" alt="Campaign type step with Win-Back Campaign selected and a name entered" />
</Frame>

### Set the trigger

Choose how long a member must be quiet before the message goes out: 14, 30, 60, or 90 days without a visit. Match it to your visit rhythm. A cafe that sees regulars weekly can act at 14 or 30 days. A salon with six-week appointment cycles should wait 60 or 90, or you'll message people who were coming back anyway.

**Maximum sends per customer** is a lifetime cap for this campaign: 1, 2, or 3 times. Once a member has had that many win-back messages from it, the campaign never writes to them again, however many times they lapse afterwards.

<Frame caption="The trigger step. The inactivity window (1) decides who qualifies, and Maximum sends per customer (2) caps how many messages one person can ever get.">
  <img src="/images/campaigns/winback-03-trigger.png" alt="Trigger step showing the inactivity period set to 30 days and maximum sends per customer set to 1 time" />
</Frame>

<Warning>
The cap starts at **1 time**, so out of the box each member gets one win-back message and no more. If you want a lapsed regular to hear from you again months later, raise it to 2 or 3 before you activate the campaign.
</Warning>

<Frame caption="Maximum sends per customer offers 1, 2, or 3 times, and opens on 1.">
  <img src="/images/campaigns/winback-07-max-sends.png" alt="The Maximum sends per customer dropdown open, with 1 time ticked above 2 times and 3 times" />
</Frame>

### Attach a reward (optional)

An inactive member needs a reason to come back, so most win-back campaigns carry one. Pick from **Free Item**, **Bonus Stamps** (1 to 20), **Bonus Points** (1 to 1,000), **Discount** (a percentage off), or **Special Badge**. Fill in the details; the wizard won't continue with a half-configured reward. You can also leave the whole step empty to send a message with no reward.

<Frame caption="Pick a reward type, or leave the step empty to send only a message.">
  <img src="/images/campaigns/winback-04-reward.png" alt="Reward step showing the five reward types" />
</Frame>

### Leave the audience on "Nobody — notification only"

The Audience step decides whether this campaign appears on your page in the Qtap app. For a win-back push, keep the default: **Nobody — notification only**. Picking **Members only** or **Everyone** turns the campaign into a claimable offer on your page instead, and Qtap stops pushing it to inactive members automatically.

### Write the message

The title fits 100 characters and the message 500. Both are required. Say what they get, plainly: "Your next coffee is on us" beats "We have an offer for you."

You can personalize with variables written in double curly braces: `{{first_name}}`, `{{customer_name}}`, `{{reward_name}}`, `{{merchant_name}}`, `{{stamps_count}}`, or `{{points_count}}`.

The tap-to-insert chips and the **Use Template** button insert these tags with both pairs of braces already in place, so there is nothing to add after clicking one. For what each tag fills with, see [Campaign messages and personalization](/merchants/campaigns/messages).

<Frame caption="Write the notification title and message. A preview shows how it will look.">
  <img src="/images/campaigns/winback-05-message.png" alt="Message step with the notification title and message fields filled in" />
</Frame>

### Skip Conditions and A/B Test

Both steps are optional, and neither changes what a win-back campaign does today. Click through them.

### Review and activate

Check the summary, then click **Activate Campaign** to start, or **Save as Draft** to finish later. Drafts sit on the Campaigns page until you activate them, and they don't count against your plan's campaign limit.

<Frame caption="Review the summary, then activate the campaign or save it as a draft.">
  <img src="/images/campaigns/winback-06-review.png" alt="Review step summarizing the campaign with the activate and draft buttons" />
</Frame>

</Steps>

## Repeat sends

A member can hear from the same win-back campaign more than once, but three things all have to be true.

They need room under **Maximum sends per customer**. At the starting value of 1, there is no second message and the other two rules never come into play.

The cooldown has to pass: twice your inactivity window, and never less than 60 days. So a 14-day or 30-day window still waits a full 60 days, and a 90-day window waits 180.

They have to go quiet again for the whole inactivity window. Any visit resets that clock, so someone who came back and kept coming back is never messaged a second time.

A customer who returned after your first message and then drifted off months later gets another nudge, as long as your cap leaves room for it.

<Warning>
If you shorten the inactivity window on an active campaign, say from 60 days to 30, members sitting between the two windows become eligible at the next check and get the notification right away.
</Warning>

## Pausing and plan limits

An active campaign runs on its own. To stop it, open **Campaigns** and click **Pause** on the campaign card; **Resume** brings it back.

Your plan sets how many campaigns can be active at once: 1 on Starter, 3 on Growth, 5 on Elite, and no limit on Franchise. At the cap, the **New Campaign** page asks you to pause an existing campaign or upgrade.
