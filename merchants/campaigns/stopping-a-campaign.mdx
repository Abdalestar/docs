A campaign has two stop buttons, and they are not interchangeable. **Pause** holds the
campaign where it is and leaves everything it has handed out alone. **Delete** removes the
campaign and takes the rewards your customers are holding with it.

Both live on the **Campaigns** page. Each card shows its status, and the tabs above the
list group your campaigns by that status.

## Pausing

Click **Pause** on the campaign card. The badge turns yellow and the campaign stops doing
anything new:

- It stops sending. Qtap checks for campaigns to run every 15 minutes and skips anything
  that is not active, so no new notifications go out.
- It stops handing out new rewards. Nobody new gets a voucher from it.
- Customers stop seeing a public offer on your merchant page, and nobody can claim it.
- A points multiplier stops multiplying. Scans go back to your normal earn rate.

Pausing also frees up a campaign slot, so you can start a different campaign without
upgrading your plan.

<Frame caption="An active campaign card. Pause (1) stops the campaign; the three-dot menu (2) holds View Stats and Delete.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/campaigns/stopping-02-active-card.png" alt="Cropped campaign card with the Pause button and the three-dot menu button outlined" />
</Frame>

<Warning>
**Pausing does not cancel rewards you have already given out.** A voucher that is already
in a customer's phone keeps working, and your staff can still redeem it at the counter.
Qtap never checks whether the campaign behind a code is paused, so the counter screen will
not warn anyone. If you need to stop honouring a reward, tell your staff; pausing the
campaign will not do it for you.
</Warning>

## Starting it again

A paused campaign keeps every setting. Click **Resume** and it goes back into the next
15-minute run.

<Frame caption="A paused campaign. The yellow Paused badge (1) and the Resume button (2) replace the Pause button.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/campaigns/stopping-05-paused-card.png" alt="Cropped campaign card with a yellow Paused badge and a Resume button outlined" />
</Frame>

Nobody gets the message twice. Qtap remembers who it has already sent this campaign to, so
resuming only reaches members it never got to. Two types can come back to the same person:
a birthday campaign returns each year, and a win-back campaign can try a lapsed customer
again up to the limit you set.

## Deleting

Open the three-dot menu on the card and choose **Delete**.

<Frame caption="The campaign card menu. View Stats opens the numbers; Delete is outlined in red.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/campaigns/stopping-03-menu.png" alt="Campaign card with its three-dot menu open, showing View Stats and a red Delete item" />
</Frame>

Read the confirmation before you click through it.

<Frame caption="The confirmation names the campaign and says it will remove 'the campaign and all its data'. That phrase covers more than the numbers.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/campaigns/stopping-04-delete-dialog.png" alt="Delete Campaign dialog with the warning sentence outlined" />
</Frame>

"All its data" means:

| What goes | What that means for you |
|---|---|
| Every reward the campaign issued | Including unredeemed ones. They vanish from your customers' phones and their codes stop working at the counter. |
| The send history | Sent, Opened, Clicked and Redeemed are gone, and so is the record of who received it. |
| The campaign itself | There is no undo and no archive. |

<Warning>
Deleting is the only one of these actions that changes what a customer already has. If
anyone is still holding a voucher from this campaign, you take it off their phone with no
notice to them. Pause instead, let the outstanding vouchers get used or expire, then delete
it later.
</Warning>

<Frame caption="Pause holds the campaign still. Delete reaches back and removes what it already gave out.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/campaigns/pause-vs-delete.svg" alt="Diagram comparing pause and delete: pause stops new sends, new claims and the multiplier while issued vouchers keep working; delete removes the campaign, its history and every voucher it issued" />
</Frame>

## Who can do this

Owners and managers. Pausing, resuming and deleting all need campaign editing rights, which
managers have by default. The Staff role cannot open the Campaigns page unless an owner
grants it.

You can also pause, resume and delete from a campaign's own page. The confirmation there is
a plain browser message that does not name the campaign, so the card menu is the safer place
to delete from.

<Note>
A campaign can also end on its own. A flash sale that reaches its redemption limit switches
itself to **Ended** and stops. There is no button that ends a campaign by hand; use Pause.
</Note>
