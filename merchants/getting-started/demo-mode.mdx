A new Qtap account is empty, which makes it hard to tell what a busy Members list or a QR code report will look like once customers start arriving. Demo mode fills every page with a sample business so you can look around, and it leaves your own records alone while you do.

## Turning it on

The switch is the eye button in the top bar, to the left of the location picker. Hover it and it says **Try demo mode**.

<Frame caption="The eye button in the top bar turns demo mode on and off.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/getting-started/demo-01-toggle.png" alt="The dashboard top bar with the demo mode eye button highlighted" />
</Frame>

Click it. A purple banner appears across the top of every page, saying "You're viewing sample data. No real data will be modified.", and a short tour opens on the sidebar.

<Frame caption="Demo mode on: the banner sits above every page, and the tour starts on the sidebar.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/getting-started/demo-02-banner-tour.png" alt="The dashboard in demo mode with the purple banner and the first tour step" />
</Frame>

On a narrow phone screen the eye button moves into the account menu in the top right corner.

## The tour

The tour walks through ten places in the dashboard, starting with the sidebar and finishing at Settings. Each step highlights the part of the screen it is describing. **Next** moves forward, the back arrow returns to the previous step, and the dots show how far along you are. Close it with the X at any point, or click anywhere on the dimmed background.

<Frame caption="Step two of the tour, with the stat cards highlighted while the tour describes them.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/getting-started/demo-03-tour-metrics.png" alt="Tour step titled Key Metrics highlighting the four dashboard stat cards" />
</Frame>

Closing the tour does not turn demo mode off. The banner stays until you exit.

## What the sample data covers

The sample business is a cafe called Brew House Qatar, with two branches and twenty customers. Members, QR Codes, NFC Tags, Redemptions and the stamp and points operations pages all read from it, and so do the figures on Analytics, Campaigns, Notifications and the staff activity log.

<Frame caption="Members in demo mode: twenty sample customers with balances and join dates.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/getting-started/demo-04-members.png" alt="The Members page in demo mode listing twenty sample customers" />
</Frame>

The sample customers have realistic histories, so the pages behave the way they will once you have members of your own. You can search the Members list, sort it, open a profile, and see what a customer looks like a few months into a loyalty program.

<Frame caption="QR Codes in demo mode, with two sample codes and their scan counts.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/getting-started/demo-05-qr-codes.png" alt="The QR Codes page in demo mode showing two sample codes with scan counts" />
</Frame>

## What demo mode does not touch

Demo mode never reads or writes your own members, cards, codes or campaigns. The pages show a fixed sample set that ships with the dashboard.

Buttons that would change something split two ways. Deleting a QR code, switching one on or off, adding a tag to a customer or assigning a location answers with a short "Demo Mode" notice instead of running. Counter actions such as issuing a stamp or awarding points point at a sample customer, and that customer does not exist in your account, so nothing reaches a real record. If one of those buttons returns an error while demo mode is on, that is why.

<Warning>
Two parts of the dashboard home keep showing your own information in demo mode: the business name in the top bar, and the Recent Activity feed. If you are showing the dashboard to someone else, be aware that the feed still lists real customer names.
</Warning>

## Turning it off

Click **Exit** on the purple banner, or click the eye button again.

<Frame caption="Exit on the banner returns the dashboard to your own data.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/getting-started/demo-06-exit.png" alt="The demo mode banner with the Exit button highlighted" />
</Frame>

Demo mode stays on until you turn it off. It survives moving between pages and reloading the browser, so if the banner is still there tomorrow, that is why. Qtap stores the setting in your browser rather than on your account, so turning it on does not change what anyone else on your team sees.

Anyone who can sign in to the dashboard can use demo mode, including staff. Nothing in it depends on your plan.
