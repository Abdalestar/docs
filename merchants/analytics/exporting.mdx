The **Export** button sits at the top right of **Analytics**, next to the time period
picker. It opens a small window called **Export Analytics Report** where you pick one
format and tick the sections you want.

<Frame caption="Export sits beside the time period picker (1). The period you have chosen is the period the file covers.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/analytics/exporting-01-button.png" alt="The top of the Analytics page with the time period picker and the Export button outlined" />
</Frame>

Every format is built inside your browser, so nothing is emailed to you and the file
never leaves your device on its way to your downloads folder.

## The three formats

<Frame caption="The export window on a points account: format on top, sections underneath.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/analytics/exporting-02-dialog-points.png" alt="The Export Analytics Report window showing PDF Report, CSV Data and Story PNG, with six section checkboxes" />
</Frame>

- **PDF Report** is the default. A cover page with your business name and a written
  summary, a contents list, then a page or two of charts and tables. This is the one to
  send to a partner or print for a meeting.
- **CSV Data** is the raw numbers, day by day, for a spreadsheet.
- **Story PNG** is a 1080 by 1920 image with your logo and your headline numbers,
  sized for an Instagram or WhatsApp story.

PDF and CSV both download as `qtap-analytics-report-` followed by the date and time.
The image downloads as `qtap-story-` and the same stamp.

## Choosing sections

Every box starts ticked, and **Export** is greyed out if you untick all of them. The
list you see depends on the kind of loyalty program you run. A points account gets
Points Overview, Points Earned Over Time, Outstanding Points Liability, Staff
Performance, Customer Segments and Insights.

<Frame caption="The same window on a stamp card account. Peak Hours replaces the points sections.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/analytics/exporting-03-dialog-stamp.png" alt="The export window on a stamp card account listing Overview Metrics, Stamps Over Time, Staff Performance, Customer Segments, Peak Hours and Insights" />
</Frame>

<Warning>
**Wait for the page to finish loading before you click Export.** The file copies
whatever the page has loaded at the moment you press the button, so a section still
showing a grey placeholder is dropped silently and Qtap still says "Export successful".
Exporting twenty seconds after opening Analytics produced a PDF with Staff Performance
and Key Insights missing; waiting for the page to settle produced the same report with
both in it. Give it half a minute and check no card is still blank.
</Warning>

## The checkboxes do not apply to every format

Unticking a section reliably keeps it out of the **CSV**. The other two formats only
read part of the list.

| Section | CSV | PDF | Story PNG |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Overview Metrics (stamp accounts) | Follows the box | Follows the box | Follows the box |
| Stamps Over Time | Follows the box | Follows the box | Never in it |
| Peak Hours | Follows the box | Follows the box | Never in it |
| Points Overview (points accounts) | Follows the box | Always in it | Follows the box |
| Points Earned Over Time | Follows the box | Always in it | Never in it |
| Outstanding Points Liability | Follows the box | Always in it | Never in it |
| Staff Performance | Follows the box | Follows the box | Never in it |
| Customer Segments | Follows the box | Follows the box | Never in it |
| Insights | Follows the box | Follows the box | First three only |

<Warning>
**A points account cannot keep its points figures out of the PDF.** The three points
boxes are read when Qtap builds the CSV and ignored when it builds the PDF. The
screenshot below is page two of a PDF exported with Points Overview, Points Earned
Over Time and Outstanding Points Liability all unticked. The written summary on the
cover quotes the same figures. If those numbers are sensitive, send the CSV with the
boxes unticked instead.
</Warning>

## What the PDF looks like

<Frame caption="The cover page of a real export. The summary paragraph and the contents list are written from your own numbers.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/analytics/exporting-04-pdf-cover.png" alt="PDF cover page reading Analytics Report, Last 30 Days, prepared for Golden Crust Bakery, with an executive summary and a four item contents list" />
</Frame>

The contents list only names the sections that made it into the file, so it doubles as
a check that you exported what you meant to.

## What the CSV looks like

The CSV is laid out as a report rather than one clean table. It opens with a banner and
your business name, then each section gets its own divider line and its own column
headings. You will need to split it up before a pivot table will work on it.

Two things to know before you start sorting:

- Dates in the daily rows read `Sep 7` and `Oct 1`, with no year. Sorting that column
  alphabetically will not put the days in order.
- Once a figure reaches a thousand, Qtap writes it with a comma and does not wrap it in
  quotes, so a spreadsheet reads `1,234` as two cells and shifts the rest of that row
  across. Staff names and insight text are quoted and come through fine.

## The Story image

<Frame caption="A real Story PNG. Your logo, your business name, the period, the headline numbers, and up to three insights.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/analytics/exporting-05-story.png" alt="A maroon Instagram Story image showing Golden Crust Bakery, Last 30 Days, four metric cards and a Key Insights box" />
</Frame>

Each insight is cut to a single line, so the longer ones end mid-sentence. Read them in
full on the Analytics page or in the PDF before you post the image.

## What never gets exported

The file covers the main Analytics page only. The Points Expiry Forecast, Top Points
Earners and Reward Popularity cards at the bottom of the page are not in any export,
and neither are Churn Risk, Best Customers or Regional Benchmarks. For a per branch or
per staff member breakdown, use the
[detailed reports](/merchants/analytics/reports-hub), which have their own filters.

The numbers match the page exactly, comparison percentages included, so the notes in
[the charts article](/merchants/analytics/charts) about how those are counted apply
here too. On a stamp card account the
[branch picker](/merchants/settings/branch-switcher) narrows most of what you export;
on a points account it does not.

## Who can export

Owners and managers. Anyone who can open Analytics can export it.

<Warning>
**Export data** in the Edit Permissions checklist does not hold anyone back. Ticking
**View analytics** on its own already lets a teammate open the page and download every
format, because nothing in Qtap checks the export permission. Treat the two boxes as
one decision until that is fixed.
</Warning>
