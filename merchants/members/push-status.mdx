A customer swears they never saw your offer. Qtap tells you part of the answer in two places: the **Push** column on the Members list, and a status banner on that customer's profile.

## The Push column

Open **Members**. The **Push** column sits between Last Active and the row menu.

<Frame caption="Three states in the Push column: a green bell (push on), a grey crossed bell (push off), and a dash on a customer who deleted their Qtap account. Names and contact details are covered here.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/members/push-status-01-list.png" alt="The Members table with the Push column outlined, showing a green bell on one row, a crossed bell on another, and a dash on a deleted member" />
</Frame>

Hover a bell and Qtap names the state: *Push notifications enabled* or *Push notifications not enabled*. On a phone the column folds into the stacked line under the customer's name, where it reads **Push: Enabled** or **Push: Not enabled**.

## The banner on a profile

Open a customer from the row menu's **View Profile**. Under their details sits a banner in one of two states.

<Frame caption="Push is on. The bell badge on the avatar (1) and the green banner (2) both say so.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/members/push-status-03-enabled.png" alt="A member profile showing a green bell badge on the avatar and a green Push Notifications Enabled banner" />
</Frame>

<Frame caption="Push is off. There is no badge on the avatar, and the banner asks you to talk to the customer.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/members/push-status-04-disabled.png" alt="A member profile showing an amber Push Notifications Disabled banner that reads Ask this customer to enable notifications in the Qtap mobile app" />
</Frame>

If the customer has muted your business, the green banner gains an amber **Muted your notifications** chip and the bell badge on the avatar turns amber. A mute is set by the customer inside the Qtap app, one business at a time. You cannot set one or clear one.

## When you cannot send at all

With push off, **Send Notification** on the profile is greyed out.

<Frame caption="The button is disabled, and hovering it explains why.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/members/push-status-05-send-blocked.png" alt="A greyed out Send Notification button with a tooltip reading This member does not have push notifications enabled" />
</Frame>

The row menu on the Members list does the same thing: **Send Notification** is replaced by a disabled **Push not enabled** item.

<Frame caption="The row menu on a customer with push off.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/members/push-status-02-row-menu.png" alt="A member row menu showing View Profile, a disabled Push not enabled item, Add Tag, and Delete Member" />
</Frame>

## What the green banner does not tell you

<Warning>
The banner reads one switch. It says *This customer will receive notifications from your campaigns*, but a campaign has three more checks to clear after that one, and the dashboard shows you none of them.
</Warning>

Four things have to line up before a campaign or a one-off push reaches a phone:

1. **A registered phone.** The customer installed the Qtap app and signed in on a device. Someone who joined by scanning a QR code at your counter has a Qtap account with no app behind it.
2. **Push switched on.** This is the one the banner and the Push column read.
3. **Suggestions & offers switched on.** A separate switch in the customer's own app settings, described there as *Nudges and merchant campaigns*. It starts off, so a customer has to go and turn it on before any campaign can reach them.
4. **Your business not muted.**

<Frame caption="A campaign clears all four checks. A receipt for something the customer just did clears the first two.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/members/push-delivery-gates.svg" alt="Diagram of the four checks a push clears, with a campaign lane needing all four and a receipt lane needing only the first two" />
</Frame>

Because the third switch starts off, most of your customers will not get a campaign on their phone until they turn it on. That is the usual reason a send shows a healthy member count and a low delivered figure.

## Receipts always get through

The message Qtap sends when a customer earns a stamp, earns points, finishes a card, redeems a reward or has their balance corrected is a receipt for something they just did. Preferences and mutes do not silence it. A receipt needs only the first two checks.

## A quiet phone is not a lost message

Whatever the outcome, Qtap writes the message into the customer's in-app inbox. Someone whose phone stayed silent still sees it the next time they open the app. That is also why your send can report a recipient it never rang.

## What you can do about it

Nothing in the dashboard turns push on for someone else, clears a mute, or flips their offers switch. Those are the customer's to set, in **Account → Settings** in the Qtap app. Ask at the counter when it matters.

There is no way to stop messaging one customer either. Campaigns go to a whole audience, so the way to leave someone out is to pick an audience they are not in.
