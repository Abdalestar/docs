The Card Designer is where you settle the look of a stamp card before you build it.
You pick a starting template, change the color, icon and stamp count, and a phone
preview updates as you go. When you are happy with it, **Save & Continue** opens the
create-card wizard with your design already filled in.

It lives at the `/cards/design` address. Nothing in the sidebar or on the Cards page
links to it, so you reach it by typing the address.

<Frame caption="The Card Designer. The four panels (1) restyle the card, and Save & Continue (2) carries the design into the create-card wizard.">
  <img src="/images/stamp-cards/card-designer-overview.png" alt="The Card Designer page with the panel tabs and the Save and Continue button highlighted" />
</Frame>

The page splits in two: the design panels on the left, and a live preview of the
member's card on the right. Four tabs sit above the panels.

## Templates

The **Templates** tab opens with six starter designs: Coffee Shop, Pizza Place,
Salon & Spa, Retail Store, Gaming Cafe, and Blank. Click one and its color, icon,
stamp count and reward text fill in at once.

<Frame caption="Six starter templates. Each one sets the color, stamp icon, stamp count and reward text together.">
  <img src="/images/stamp-cards/card-designer-templates.png" alt="The Start from Template gallery with six template tiles" />
</Frame>

<Warning>
  Picking a template replaces everything you have already changed, including your
  reward text. Choose your template first, then make your edits.
</Warning>

## Colors

The **Colors** tab sets one value: **Card Color**, the background of the card in the
Qtap app. Click the swatch for a color picker with eight presets, or type a hex value
into the field beside it.

<Frame caption="The Colors panel sets a single card color, by picker, preset, or hex value.">
  <img src="/images/stamp-cards/card-designer-colors.png" alt="The Colors panel showing the card color swatch, hex field, and an open color picker with eight presets" />
</Frame>

## Icons

The **Icons** tab sets the stamp icon, the shape that fills in each time a member
earns a stamp. Twelve icons are on offer and the selected one gets a maroon border.
If none of them suit you, you can upload your own picture on the wizard's Card Design
step after you continue.

<Frame caption="Twelve stamp icons. The selected icon is outlined in maroon.">
  <img src="/images/stamp-cards/card-designer-icons.png" alt="The Stamp Icon panel with twelve icons in a grid" />
</Frame>

## Settings

The **Settings** tab holds two fields:

- **Stamps to Collect**: 6, 8, 10, 12, 15, or 20.
- **Reward Text**: the reward a member earns when the card is full, such as *Free pizza on your 8th visit!*

<Frame caption="Card Settings: how many stamps fill the card (1), and the reward at the end of it (2).">
  <img src="/images/stamp-cards/card-designer-settings.png" alt="The Card Settings panel with the Stamps to Collect dropdown and the Reward Text field" />
</Frame>

## The live preview

The right side shows a phone with your card on it, using your business name and a few
example stamps already collected. Every change on the left updates here, so you can
read the card the way a member will before you commit to it.

<Frame caption="The preview after picking the Pizza Place template: the card color, stamp icon, stamp count and reward text all change together.">
  <img src="/images/stamp-cards/card-designer-preview.png" alt="A phone preview of a red loyalty card with pizza stamp icons and the reward text" />
</Frame>

## What carries into the wizard

**Save & Continue** opens the create-card wizard at `/cards/new` with four things
already set from your design: the stamp count, the card color, the stamp icon, and
the reward text.

<Frame caption="The wizard opens with the design applied: 8 stamps (1), and the red card and pizza icons in the preview.">
  <img src="/images/stamp-cards/card-designer-handoff-design.png" alt="The Create Stamp Card wizard with the stamps field set to 8 and a red pizza-themed card in the preview" />
</Frame>

Your reward text arrives as a ready-made **main reward** on the wizard's Rewards step,
set to trigger when the card is full. You can rename it, change it, or add more
rewards beside it.

<Frame caption="The reward text from the designer becomes the card's main reward, due after 8 stamps.">
  <img src="/images/stamp-cards/card-designer-handoff-rewards.png" alt="The wizard Rewards step showing a main reward named Free pizza on your 8th visit, after 8 stamps" />
</Frame>

You still name the card, write its description, choose its branches and publish it in
the wizard. The designer sets the look, not the whole card.

<Note>
  The handoff happens once. Your design is held in the browser until the wizard reads
  it, so if you reload the wizard or go back to the designer, set it up again. Nothing
  is stored on your account until you publish the card, and a design is only applied
  to a brand-new card, never to one you are editing.
</Note>

For the fields you set when you actually create a card, see [Creating a stamp
card](/merchants/stamp-cards/creating) and [Designing your stamp
card](/merchants/stamp-cards/designing).

## Who can open it

Owners and managers can open the Card Designer. Staff have no access to the Cards area
by default, so they do not see it unless you give them stamp card access.

The designer is for stamp cards only. On a points account the page answers **Access
Denied**, even for the owner, and the sidebar shows Points in place of Cards.
