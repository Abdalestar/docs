When a customer taps their phone on one of your NFC tags, Qtap reads the address written on the tag and runs the action you set for it. That tap adds a stamp, awards points, or records a check-in, then logs the visit against the customer's account.

  <img src="/images/merchants/nfc-tags/nfc-tap-list.png" alt="NFC Tags page with the Action and Taps columns called out" />
</Frame>

## A tap always belongs to a customer

A customer taps with the Qtap app open, so every tap credits a real account. If the app can't identify the customer, the tap doesn't register. The first time someone taps one of your tags, Qtap adds them to your members and then runs the action, so you never have to sign them up by hand.

## What each action does

You choose one action per tag when you register it (see [NFC Tags](/merchants/nfc-tags)). A tap runs that action:

<Steps>

### Add Stamp

Adds one stamp to the stamp card linked to the tag. A tap adds one stamp per card per customer per day, so a customer can't tap twice in a row to collect extra. If the tag has no stamp card linked, the tap fails and tells the customer to ask staff.

### Award Points

Adds the points value set on the tag to the customer's balance.

### Check-In

Records the visit without adding a stamp or points. Use it when you only want to track who came in.

</Steps>

You set the action when you register a tag, and you can change it later from the tag's detail page.

## Where to see tap activity

Open **NFC Tags**, then choose **View Details** on any tag. The detail page shows four cards at the top with the Total Taps, the action, the assigned location, and the tag's status, followed by a Details panel with the serial number, purchase date, and last tap.

<Frame caption="A tag's detail page: the running tap count, the action it runs, its location and status, and the tag's details.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/nfc-tags/nfc-tap-detail.png" alt="NFC tag detail page showing Total Taps, Action, location, status, and details" />
</Frame>

The Total Taps number is the figure to watch for how often a tag gets used. It also shows next to each tag on the main list, and the summary cards there add up taps across every tag.

## How a tap differs from a QR scan

A tap and a scan run the same rules. Points multipliers apply to both, a completed card sends the same "you completed your card" message either way, and a birthday or milestone reward the customer is owed is handed over on a tap as readily as on a scan.

<Warning>
**A stamp tap is capped at one per card per day.** That limit is on taps only: a QR scan or a code typed in by staff has no daily cap. The day rolls over at midnight UTC, which is 3am in Qatar.
</Warning>

The other difference is who can use them. A tap needs the Qtap app on the customer's phone, while a QR code opens for anyone with a camera. For a customer who has not installed the app yet, point them at a QR code.
