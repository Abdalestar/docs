Your Qtap page has one web address: `dashboard.qtap.qa/m/` followed by a short name for your business. Put it in your Instagram bio, or send it to a customer on WhatsApp. It opens your page in the Qtap app for anyone who has the app, and in a browser for everyone else.

The address lives in the **Public link** card, the first card on the Merchant Page screen. Open **Merchants** in the sidebar, then click **Edit Merchant**.

</Frame>

## Where the name comes from

Qtap built the name from your business name the day you signed up, in lowercase with hyphens between the words. "Golden Crust Bakery" becomes `golden-crust-bakery`. If another business had already taken that exact name, Qtap adds a number to the end, so the second Golden Crust Bakery gets `golden-crust-bakery-2`.

## Copy the link

Click the copy button and the icon turns into a green tick for a couple of seconds. Some browsers block a page from writing to the clipboard. When yours does, Qtap puts the address in a message instead so you can select it by hand.

## Change the name

Click the pencil to edit the part after `/m/`. Type the name you want, wait for the check under the field, then click **Save**.

<Frame caption="A free name reads Available under the field, and Save turns on.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/merchant-page/public-link-available.png" alt="Editing the public link with an available name and the Save button enabled" />
</Frame>

The rules are 3 to 48 characters, lowercase letters, numbers, and single hyphens between words. The field cleans up what you type as you go: capitals drop to lowercase and anything else is left out. If a name will not work, the reason appears where **Available** would be:

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Already taken | Another business has this one. Pick a different name. |
| This name is reserved | Qtap keeps some names for its own pages, such as `settings` or `support`. Pick a different name. |
| Must be 3-48 characters | Lengthen or shorten the name. |
| Only lowercase letters, numbers and single hyphens | Usually a double hyphen, or a hyphen at the start or end. |

<Frame caption="Qtap gives the reason under the field and leaves Save switched off.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/merchant-page/public-link-reserved.png" alt="The public link editor refusing a reserved name" />
</Frame>

## What a change breaks

Save opens a confirmation first, with the address you are about to move to.

<Frame caption="The confirmation names the new address and what happens to the old one.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/merchant-page/public-link-confirm.png" alt="Change your public link confirmation dialog" />
</Frame>

<Warning>
The old address stops working the moment you confirm. Anything already printed or posted with it lands on a dead page: flyers, your social bio, messages you sent last week, paid ads. Settle on the name before the artwork goes to the printer.
</Warning>

Two things survive a change, so you do not need to redo them:

- **Printed QR codes and NFC tags.** They carry their own scan address, which does not include your page name.
- **Push notifications.** Qtap fills in the current name each time it sends one, so a message that opens your page keeps working.

<Note>
Only owners and managers can open the Merchant Page screen, so only they can change the link. The screen has no sidebar entry of its own, and the **Merchants** page that leads to it is owner-only. A manager who needs to edit the page has to be sent the `/merchant-page` address directly.
</Note>
