---
title: "Editing or ending a live offer"
sidebarTitle: "Editing a live offer"

---

An offer stays fully editable until the first customer claims it. After that, Qtap locks
the parts of the deal your customers already agreed to and lets you change everything else.

## Check the claims count first

Open **Campaigns > Public offers**. Each row shows how many vouchers customers have
claimed, so you can tell at a glance whether an offer is still open to free editing.

<Frame caption="The claims count on each row tells you whether an offer is still fully editable.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/offers/editing-01-list.png" alt="The Public offers list with the claims count and Pause button on one row" />
</Frame>

To open an offer, click its title or use the three-dot menu and choose **Edit**.

<Frame caption="The three-dot menu on each offer row.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/offers/editing-02-menu.png" alt="Offer row menu showing Edit and Delete" />
</Frame>

## What locks after the first claim

Once at least one voucher is out, a gold banner at the top of the edit form tells you how
many customers hold it, and the **Voucher title** field turns read-only.

<Frame caption="Once customers hold a voucher, the deal they claimed is locked.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/offers/editing-03-locked-banner.png" alt="Edit offer page showing the locked banner and a read-only voucher title" />
</Frame>

The reward type and its value lock too. Unavailable types grey out, and the one customers
claimed stays selected.

<Frame caption="The reward type is frozen at whatever customers claimed.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/offers/editing-04-reward-locked.png" alt="Reward type grid with all options greyed out except the selected one" />
</Frame>

Your colour, cover image, badge label, description and audience stay editable the whole
time. If you need a different deal, leave this one alone and create a new offer.

## Dates and limits only stretch

Timing and quantity fields still open, but they move in one direction: whatever a customer
was promised can get better, never worse.

An **end date** can be pushed later. The field refuses any earlier date.

<Frame caption="An existing end date can move later, never earlier.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/offers/editing-05-end-date.png" alt="End date field with the note: can be extended, not brought forward" />
</Frame>

An offer that never had an end date stays open-ended, so the field is disabled rather than
letting you add a closing date to a promise that had none.

<Frame caption="An open-ended offer cannot gain an end date while customers hold it.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/offers/editing-08-open-ended.png" alt="Disabled end date field reading: open-ended while customers hold it" />
</Frame>

**Voucher valid for** works the same way. The dropdown hides any window shorter than the
current one, so validity can grow but not shrink.

<Frame caption="Shorter validity windows disappear from the list once vouchers are out.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/offers/editing-06-valid-for.png" alt="Voucher valid for dropdown showing only the current window and longer options" />
</Frame>

**Total claims available** cannot drop below the number already claimed.

<Frame caption="The claim limit stops at the number customers have already taken.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/offers/editing-07-claim-total.png" alt="Total claims field showing it cannot go below 11 already claimed" />
</Frame>

<Note>
These rules live in the database, not just the form, so they hold no matter how the change
reaches Qtap. If a locked value does slip through, the save fails and the reason appears in
a **Could not save** message.
</Note>

For comparison, an offer nobody has claimed yet gives you every field back, including the
title and reward type.

<Frame caption="With no claims yet, every field on the offer stays editable.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/offers/editing-09-unlocked.png" alt="Edit page for an offer with no claims, all fields editable" />
</Frame>

## Taking an offer down

**Pause** is the way to stop a live offer. It takes the offer off your merchant page right
away and keeps it in your list, so you can resume it later.

<Frame caption="A paused offer stays in your list and comes off your public page until you resume it.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/offers/editing-11-paused.png" alt="A paused offer showing the Paused badge and Resume button" />
</Frame>

**Delete** removes the offer for good. Qtap asks you to confirm, and there is no undo.

<Frame caption="Deleting asks for confirmation and cannot be reversed.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/offers/editing-10-delete.png" alt="Delete offer confirmation dialog" />
</Frame>

<Note>
There is no separate End button. When an offer's end date passes, customers stop seeing it
on your page, but your list still shows the offer as **Active**. Pause it if you want the
list to match what customers see.
</Note>

<Warning>
Pausing or deleting an offer does not cancel vouchers customers already claimed. Those keep
their own expiry date and your staff can still redeem them at the counter. Honour them, or
contact the customer before you remove the offer.
</Warning>

## Who can edit offers

Owners and managers can edit, pause and delete offers. Staff have no campaigns access by
default, so they never see the Public offers page unless you grant it. See
[Roles and permissions](/merchants/staff/roles-permissions) to change that.
