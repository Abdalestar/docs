Qtap gives every merchant a set of ready-made marketing materials, so you never have to design your own signage to promote your loyalty program. Everything is already on brand and sized correctly for its purpose.

## What is in the library

| Asset | Format | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Social media posts | 1080 x 1080 PNG | Instagram and Facebook posts announcing your loyalty program |
| Posters | A4 and A5 PDF | Counter and wall signage in your shop |
| Counter sticker | 100 x 60 mm PDF | Next to the register, where people pay |
| Window decal | 130 mm circle PDF | Your door or window, so customers know you are on Qtap |
| Staff instructions | A4 PDF | A one-page counter guide for your team |
| Counter setup sheet | A5 PDF | How to place and use your QR code and NFC tag |

Social posts and posters come in industry versions for cafes, restaurants, and salons and barbershops, plus a general version that works for any business. More industries are on the way.

## A simple first week

1. Put the window decal on your door.
2. Print two posters and place one at the counter and one on a wall.
3. Post one social image from your industry pack on Instagram.
4. Give the staff instructions sheet to your team.
