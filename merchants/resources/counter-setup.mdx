description: "Place your code where people pay, and every scan takes care of itself."
---

This is how customers earn at your counter. Set it up once and it runs itself.

## 1. Generate your code

Go to **QR Codes** in the sidebar and generate a reusable code. Reusable codes are permanent, made for counters and signage, and never expire. Print it at a size people can scan from arm's length.

For deliveries and events, use **QR Codes → Batch** to generate between 25 and 500 one-time codes and download them as a sticker PDF. Each one works exactly once.

## 2. Place it next to the register

Eye level, close to where people pay. The counter sticker and poster from the [marketing asset library](/merchants/resources/marketing-asset-library) are designed to hold your printed code.

If you use NFC tags, manage them under **NFC Tags** in the sidebar. A tag has no battery and nothing to charge. It only stores a link to your business, and all fraud checks run on Qtap's servers.

## 3. Customers scan or tap

A scan or a tap awards the stamp or points. New customers can scan before they create an account, and their progress is saved when they sign up.

Every scan is logged with time, location, and member, and shows up in your dashboard.

## Good to know

- If a tag goes missing, disable it from **NFC Tags** and order a new one.
- A single code can award stamps, award points, check a customer in, or trigger a reward, depending on how you configure it.
- On Elite and Franchise plans, QR codes can be branded with your business colors.
