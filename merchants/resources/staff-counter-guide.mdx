Your staff need to know two things: how customers earn, and how to hand over a reward. Both take seconds. A printable A4 version of this page is available in the [marketing asset library](/merchants/resources/marketing-asset-library).

## How customers earn

**Stamp cards.** One stamp per visit. The customer scans the QR code at the counter or taps their phone on the NFC tag. Staff do nothing. A daily cap and a short delay between stamps stop anyone from stamping twice.

**Points programs.** Points are based on what the customer spends, for example 1 QAR = 1 point. The customer scans the same code and the amount is confirmed from the dashboard. Their balance updates in the app right away.

Stamp activity appears under **Stamp Ops** in the sidebar, and points activity under **Points Ops**.

## Confirming a redemption

1. The customer shows their reward in the app. It appears as a 6-character code or a QR code.
2. Open **Redemptions** in the dashboard and enter the code. If the customer cannot open the app, open **Members** and look them up by phone number or email instead.
3. Hand over the reward. The app updates on its own, and the redemption is logged under the staff member who processed it.

## Quick answers for customers

- **"I don't have the app yet."** They can scan the QR code first and sign up after. Their progress is saved.
- **"My card is full, what now?"** The reward is waiting in their app. After they redeem it, the card resets and they keep earning.
- **"Can I stamp twice?"** No. The system enforces a daily cap and a delay between stamps.
- **"The code won't scan."** Use member lookup in **Members** by phone or email instead.

## What staff accounts can do

Team members with the Staff role can issue stamps and points, process redemptions, and look up members. They cannot change settings or billing. Invite your team from **Staff** in the sidebar, and see their activity under **Staff → Activity**.
