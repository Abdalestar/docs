title: "Turning off Qtap's marketing emails"
sidebarTitle: "Marketing emails"
description: "Stop trial guidance, feature spotlights, and the weekly digest without losing your receipts."
---

Qtap sends your business two kinds of email. Receipts, password resets, and billing notices go out whatever your settings say. Trial guidance and the weekly digest are optional, and one switch turns them off.

The switch lives on **Settings → Notifications**, below the email and push toggles.

<Frame caption="The Marketing emails card (1) sits between the Email Notifications and Push Notifications cards.">
  <img src="/images/settings/marketing-emails-page.png" alt="The Notification Preferences page with the Marketing emails card outlined" />
</Frame>

## What the switch covers

**Send marketing emails** is on for every account. Turning it off stops:

- The trial sequence: a welcome message on your first day, a progress note around day 7, and a reminder around day 12.
- Feature spotlights sent during a trial.
- The weekly digest, a Monday summary of your program that goes to Elite and Franchise accounts.
- The note that arrives when Qtap adds days to your trial. You still get the extra days; only the email stops.

<Frame caption="The card names what the switch covers. Receipts and security notices sit outside it.">
  <img src="/images/settings/marketing-emails-switch.png" alt="The Marketing emails card showing the Send marketing emails switch turned on" />
</Frame>

These keep arriving with the switch off:

- Payment receipts and failed-payment warnings.
- Confirmation when you subscribe, upgrade, downgrade, or cancel.
- Add-on purchase confirmations.
- Password resets and staff invitations.

## Turning it off

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings and choose Notifications">
    Only the owner can open Settings. Managers and staff do not see it unless the owner gives them settings access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch off Send marketing emails">
    The **Save Preferences** button in the top right wakes up as soon as you change anything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save Preferences">
    Nothing changes until you do. Leaving the page without saving keeps the old setting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="With the switch off (1), Save Preferences (2) turns from grey to maroon. The change is not stored until you click it.">
  <img src="/images/settings/marketing-emails-off.png" alt="The Notification Preferences page with the marketing switch off and the Save Preferences button active" />
</Frame>

## The unsubscribe link in the email

Every marketing email carries **Manage email preferences or unsubscribe** at the bottom. The unsubscribe link asks you to confirm on a web page before it saves anything, so a mail app that quietly opens links in the background cannot unsubscribe you by mistake.

Confirming there does the same thing as the switch. To start receiving these emails again, turn the switch back on in Settings.

<Frame caption="The unsubscribe link opens a confirm page. Only the button on that page saves the change.">
  <img src="/images/settings/marketing-email-unsubscribe-flow.svg" alt="Diagram of the unsubscribe link: the email footer leads to a confirm page, and the Unsubscribe button saves the change" />
</Frame>

<Warning>
  One trial email ignores the switch. In your last three days of trial, Qtap emails you a daily countdown of how many days are left, and it sends whether marketing email is on or off. The message carries an unsubscribe link that will not stop it. Watch your trial end date on **Settings → Billing** instead of relying on that email.
</Warning>

## Who it applies to

The setting belongs to your business, not to one person. Qtap sends these emails to the owner's login address, so switching it off stops them for the account rather than for a single teammate.

<Note>
  The **Weekly Report** toggle in the Email Notifications card above is a separate control, and it is not the weekly digest this page describes. See [Notifications](/merchants/settings/notifications) for what those toggles cover.
</Note>
