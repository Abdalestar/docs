Settings → Notifications holds six switches that promise alerts about your own business, such as an email whenever a customer joins your program. Your choices save to your account, and nothing sends on the back of them yet. A seventh switch on the same page, **Send marketing emails**, is the one control here that changes what Qtap sends you. This page explains what the six really do today and which emails you actually receive.

<Frame caption="Settings → Notifications: the Email card (1), the Marketing emails card (2), and the Push card (3). Save Preferences stays grey until you change a switch.">
  <img src="/images/settings/settings-notifications-overview.png" alt="The Notification Preferences page showing the Email Notifications card, the Marketing emails card, and the Push Notifications card" />
</Frame>

<Warning>
None of these six switches is wired to a sender. Qtap stores your choice and reads it back the next time you open the page, and no part of the product asks it whether to email or push you. Turning them all on will not bring you a single new-member alert, and turning them all off will not stop anything you are getting today. Watch **Recent Activity** on your dashboard home for joins and redemptions instead.
</Warning>

Only the owner reaches this page. Managers and teammates get **settings** access turned off by default, and nothing in the Edit Permissions dialog turns it on.

## The email switches

<Frame caption="The three email switches, showing the state a new account starts in: New Member Sign-ups on, Reward Redemptions off, Weekly Report on.">
  <img src="/images/settings/settings-notifications-email.png" alt="Email Notifications card with the New Member Sign-ups, Reward Redemptions, and Weekly Report switches" />
</Frame>

**New Member Sign-ups** is on when you first open the page. **Reward Redemptions** is off. **Weekly Report** is on.

The Weekly Report switch is the near miss of the three. Qtap does send a weekly insights email, on Monday mornings, but it reads a different setting than the one this switch writes, and it only goes to accounts on **Elite** or **Franchise**. So a Growth or Starter account can leave Weekly Report on forever and never get the email, and an Elite account gets it whether the switch is on or off. There is no screen that turns the real one off; ask support if you want it stopped.

## The push switches

<Frame caption="The three push switches. Low Stock Alerts is the odd one: Qtap has no reward stock to run low.">
  <img src="/images/settings/settings-notifications-push.png" alt="Push Notifications card with New Member Sign-ups, Reward Redemptions, and Low Stock Alerts switches" />
</Frame>

**New Member Sign-ups** starts off. **Reward Redemptions** and **Low Stock Alerts** start on.

Qtap never pushes a notification to you. Every push the platform sends goes to a customer's phone through the Qtap app, aimed at that customer's device. There is no merchant-side push, in a browser or anywhere else, so no browser permission prompt will help.

**Low Stock Alerts** goes a step further: rewards in Qtap have no stock count. You set what a reward is and what it costs in stamps or points, and nothing anywhere tracks how many are left. The switch is a leftover with no feature behind it.

## Saving

Changes do not save on their own. Flip a switch and **Save Preferences** wakes up in the top right, with a marker pinned to the bottom corner until you click it.

## What Qtap does email you

Your account still gets mail, just not from those six switches. All of it goes to the owner's login address. One card on this page does change what arrives: **Marketing emails**, sitting between the two switch cards. Turning it off stops the trial sequence, feature spotlights, and the weekly digest. [Turning off Qtap's marketing emails](/merchants/settings/marketing-emails) walks through it. Everything else keeps coming whatever you do here.

Qtap rewrote every one of these subject lines in September 2026, so older mail in your inbox reads differently.

| Email | When it arrives | Stopped by Marketing emails |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your *plan* trial is live | The day your trial starts | Yes |
| Seven days left, and the counter is empty | Day 7, when nobody has joined yet | Yes |
| *N* customers in seven days | Day 7, once members have joined | Yes |
| Two days left, *your business* | Day 12 of the trial | Yes |
| Three days left on your trial | Each of the last three days of a trial | No |
| You earned three more days | Qtap adds days to your trial | Yes |
| You are on Qtap *plan* | Your first paid subscription starts | No |
| You are on *plan* | You move up a plan | No |
| Your plan is now *plan* | You move down a plan | No |
| Your *plan* plan is canceled | A subscription ends | No |
| Receipt · $49.00 for Qtap *plan* | Every successful charge | No |
| Your card was declined | A card is declined | No |
| *Add-on name* added | You buy an add-on | No |
| Your week: *N* members, *N* rewards | Monday mornings, Elite and Franchise only | Yes |
| AI access to your data is paused | Your plan drops below Elite while Claude or ChatGPT is connected | No |
| Your AI keys reset on *date* | About a week before a connected AI tool needs signing in again | No |

<Warning>
  **Three days left on your trial** shows an unsubscribe link, and that link does not stop it. The email runs on its own daily schedule and never reads the Marketing emails switch, so it keeps arriving until the trial ends. Watch your trial end date in [Billing](/merchants/settings/billing) instead.
</Warning>

The last two rows only reach accounts that have connected Claude or ChatGPT to their analytics.

Password resets and staff invitations arrive by email too. Those are covered in [Resetting a forgotten password](/merchants/settings/password-reset) and [Inviting a team member](/merchants/staff/inviting).
