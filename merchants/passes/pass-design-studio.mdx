When a customer adds your loyalty card to Apple Wallet, the pass takes your card colour and
your business logo. The pass design studio lets you set that appearance on purpose instead
of inheriting it, and push the change to passes that are already on customers' phones.

## Opening the studio

Go to **Cards** (or **Points** on a points account). Each programme in the list shows two
previews side by side. The left one is the card as it looks inside the Qtap app. The right
one, labelled **Wallet pass**, opens the design studio.

Two previews sit at the top right. **Default (no branding)** is the pass as it looks today,
and **Your design** updates as you change things. The stamp count and points balance in both
are sample numbers, not a real customer's.

## Colours

**Background colour** is the whole face of the pass. Leave it and the pass falls back to
your programme's card colour.

**Label colour** is used for the small caps headings like STAMPS and for the next-reward
line at the bottom. It starts on the Qtap gold.

<Frame caption="Type a hex code or pick from the swatch. Background colour (1) and label colour (2) are the only two colours you choose.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/passes/pass-design-colors.png" alt="The Colors panel with a brown background colour and a gold label colour" />
</Frame>

You never pick the main text colour. Qtap works it out from your background so the business
name and the counter stay readable.

The label colour has the same protection. Pick one that is too close to your background and
Qtap swaps it for black or white, whichever reads better, and shows a short **Adjusted for
legibility** note next to the field.

<Frame caption="A gold label on a gold background cannot be read, so Qtap replaced it with near-black and said so (1). The field now holds the colour it will actually save (2).">
  <img src="/images/merchants/passes/pass-design-contrast.png" alt="The Colors panel showing the Adjusted for legibility note after a low-contrast label colour was entered" />
</Frame>

The same check runs again when you publish, so a pass never ships with text nobody can read.

<Frame caption="The two previews after setting a brown background. The business name reads white, and the gold label carries the STAMPS heading and the reward line.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/passes/pass-design-preview-pair.png" alt="Default indigo Wallet pass beside a brown branded version of the same stamp card" />
</Frame>

## Logo and images

**Logo** is the round mark in the pass header. It uses your business logo unless you upload
something else here. Files must be PNG or JPEG and under 1 MB. Once you have uploaded one, a
**Use default** button appears to put your business logo back.

**Watermark logo** does not appear on the Wallet pass at all. It is used on your vouchers and
your merchant page, and it sits in this studio because it is the same kind of asset.

**Strip artwork** is the band across the middle of the pass. Qtap generates it from your
programme and updates it as a customer's progress changes, so there is nothing to edit.

## Publishing

Nothing is saved until you press **Publish pass design**, so leaving the page drops your
changes. The line above the button tells you how many passes are already in customers'
wallets and will be updated.

Publishing writes the design and then pushes it out. A pass registered for updates on a
phone changes within moments. Any other pass picks up the new look the next time it syncs.

<Frame caption="The same programme on a points account. The balance and the next reward sit on the pass face.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/passes/pass-design-points.png" alt="Default and custom Wallet pass previews for a points programme showing a 250 points balance" />
</Frame>

<Note>
Owners and managers can change a pass design. A teammate on the Staff role has no access to
cards or points programmes by default, so the studio is closed to them.
</Note>

Colours and rewards for the card itself are set elsewhere. See
[Editing a stamp card](/merchants/stamp-cards/editing) or
[Editing a points program](/merchants/points/editing) for those.
