When a customer adds your loyalty card to Apple Wallet, they see a pass with your
colors and your logo on it. The Pass design studio is where you set that. Every
stamp card and every points program has its own pass design, so a cafe running
both can style them differently.

## Opening the studio

Go to **Cards** (or **Points** on a points account). Each program shows two
previews side by side. The one on the right is labeled **Wallet pass**. Click it.

<Frame caption="On the Cards page, the Wallet pass preview opens the design studio for that program.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/passes/pass-entry.png" alt="A stamp card row with the Wallet pass preview highlighted" />
</Frame>

The studio puts your controls on the left and two live previews on the right.
**Default (no branding)** shows what customers get today if you have never saved a
design. **Your design** updates as you change things.

<Frame caption="The Pass design studio: color and logo controls (1), the before and after previews (2), and the publish panel (3).">
  <img src="/images/merchants/passes/pass-studio-overview.png" alt="The Pass design studio with the Colors card, the two previews, and the publish button" />
</Frame>

<Note>
  The numbers on the previews are samples, not a real customer's balance. A stamp
  pass shows a few stamps against your card's goal, and a points pass shows 250
  points, so you can judge the layout before anything is published.
</Note>

## Choosing colors

The **Colors** card has two fields.

<Frame caption="Background color (1) fills the pass. Label color (2) is used for the small caps labels and the next-reward line.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/passes/pass-colors.png" alt="The Colors card showing the background color and label color fields" />
</Frame>

**Background color** fills the whole pass. Type a hex code, or click the swatch to
open the picker. The small swatches under the picker are pulled from your
organization logo, so the suggestions already match your branding.

<Frame caption="The color picker, with suggested swatches taken from your organization logo.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/passes/pass-color-picker.png" alt="A color picker with four suggested brand swatches beneath it" />
</Frame>

**Label color** is used for the small caps labels like "STAMPS" and for the
next-reward line at the bottom of the pass.

You never pick the main text color. Qtap works it out from your background,
choosing near-white or near-black depending on which one reads better.

### Colors that cannot be read get fixed for you

If your label color would not be readable against your background, Qtap swaps it
for one that is and shows **Adjusted for legibility** next to the field. Here the
background was set to a pale cream, so the default gold label was replaced with
near-black.

<Frame caption="Setting a pale background pushed the gold label below the readable threshold, so Qtap replaced it.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/passes/pass-contrast.png" alt="The Colors card showing the label color snapped to near-black with an Adjusted for legibility note" />
</Frame>

Qtap runs the same check again when you publish, so a hard-to-read pass cannot
reach a customer even if something goes wrong in the browser.

## Your logo

The **Logo** card sets the image in the pass header. Leave it alone and your
organization logo is used.

<Frame caption="Upload a logo to override the organization logo for this program only.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/passes/pass-logo.png" alt="The Logo card with the current logo and an Upload button" />
</Frame>

Uploads must be PNG or JPEG and under 1 MB. Once you have uploaded one, a **Use
default** button appears next to Upload so you can go back to your organization
logo.

The **Watermark logo** card below it does something else. That image goes on your
vouchers and your merchant page, and it never appears on the Wallet pass, which
has no background layer to put it on.

## What you cannot change here

Strip artwork is the band across the middle of the pass, and Qtap regenerates it as
a customer's progress changes. You cannot edit it in the studio.

<Frame caption="Strip artwork is generated automatically and has no controls.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/passes/pass-strip-locked.png" alt="The Strip artwork card explaining that strip artwork updates automatically" />
</Frame>

## Publishing

Compare the two previews before you commit. Put your branding next to the plain
version and you can see exactly what a customer gains.

<Frame caption="Default appearance on the left, a custom brown background on the right.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/passes/pass-compare.png" alt="Two stamp card wallet passes side by side, one indigo and one brown" />
</Frame>

A points pass lays out differently. The balance sits over the strip instead of in
the header, and the next-reward line uses your label color.

<Frame caption="The same controls on a points program, showing the balance over the strip.">
  <img src="/images/merchants/passes/pass-points-compare.png" alt="Two points wallet passes side by side, one indigo and one navy" />
</Frame>

Above the **Publish pass design** button, Qtap tells you how many passes are already
in customers' wallets, so you know the size of what you are about to change. If
nobody has added this program to their wallet yet, it says so and only future
passes are affected.

<Warning>
  There is no draft and no separate save. **Publish pass design** saves your
  design and pushes it to every pass already in a customer's wallet. Passes that
  Qtap cannot reach right away pick the change up the next time the customer opens
  them.
</Warning>

After publishing you get a confirmation showing how many passes were updated and
how many devices were reached.

## Who can edit a pass design

Owners and managers can design and publish passes. Staff cannot: they have no
access to stamp cards or points programs by default, so the studio does not open
for them.

If you give a team member view-only access to your cards or programs, they can open
the studio and try colors, but publishing fails with a permissions error. Give
them edit access on that program type if you want them to publish.

<Note>
  This studio covers the Apple Wallet pass. The subtitle at the top of the page
  names the program you are editing and says so.
</Note>
